==== HOW TO BUILD ===

1. Undo all the seams of a thin clothing article you wish to plate

2. Print a tessilated version of Panel.STL on an FDM printer half way then pause the print, ensure to space each plate by about a quarter inch, ensure to shape the tessilation to the thin unseamed fabric

3. Place the unseamed clothing article atop the half done print (outside pointing upwards), ensure to clamp down all edges as to not have the fabric move while printing

4.  Resume the print

5.  Sew in a layer of sponge on the inside of the clothing article

6. Resew the seams of your plated and padded clothing article

7. Don't sue me and enjoy not having your ribs and/or other bones broken as easily by non lethal rounds

Note: Reccomended to print in a strong, low tempurature material
